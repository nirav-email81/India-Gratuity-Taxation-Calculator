Feature 1 — Taxation:
- Keep only the new 2025 rules (Code on Social Security).
- Add a government/private on/off switch.
  - Government = gratuity fully tax-exempt.
  - Private = compute tax on the gratuity portion only, asking for the user's
    tax bracket (auto-detect from taxable annual income excluding gratuity, with
    manual override). Show total gratuity, applicable tax bracket, tax component,
    and in-hand amount (what the employer is likely to pay after TDS).
- Include a "gratuity received earlier" input (default 0) that reduces the
  lifetime Rs 20,00,000 exemption. Approved model (Option 1):
    exempt = max(0, 20,00,000 - prior)
    excess = max(0, gratuity - exempt)
    tax = income>0 ? tax(income + excess) - tax(income) : excess x manual rate
    in-hand = gratuity - tax
  The tax on the excess must be computed EXACTLY on the bracket(s) that
  (income + excess) reaches — never on the income-only bracket (e.g. income 12L +
  excess 10L = 22L total, so the top bracket is 25% and tax = tax(22L) - tax(12L)
  = 1.9L, not 10L x 15%). The manual rate dropdown is a fallback only when income
  is left 0.
- Surcharge on high incomes (from total income = annual income + taxable gratuity
  excess): >50 lakh = 10%, >1 crore = 15%, >2 crore = 25%, >5 crore = 37% (old
  regime; new regime caps at 25%). Surcharge is a % of the income-tax amount on
  the gratuity, with marginal relief near thresholds. Show it in a separate box
  under the result table and include the surcharge in total tax and in-hand.
- Present the user's result as the same table used in the approved examples
  (Current | Prior | Exempt | Excess | Rate | Tax | In-hand).
- Show the example calculations table on the page.
- Mention prior-gratuity declaration and Form I submission during the gratuity
  process.
- Keep form light: default income/prior fields to 0.
- Make the site fully mobile-friendly: responsive page layouts on all pages and
  horizontally scrollable tables (table-wrap) on small screens.

Post-build refinements:
- Below-5-years flag: permanent <5 yrs / fixed-term <1 yr shows a reference-only
  amber banner (#eligWarning); payout can be zero.
- CTC-research: no statutory gratuity below 5 years; a real monthly "gratuity"
  salary deduction is unlawful (refundable in full & final as a wrongful salary
  deduction, not as gratuity) vs a CTC provision (~4.81% basic p.a.) which is not
  refundable. Added to learn.html caveat c1 and the KB.
- Field-aware validation (zero allowed for income/prior/DA/allowances; error only
  for service <= 0 or wage base <= 0).
- Government CCS formula: 1/4 of monthly emoluments per completed 6-month period,
  capped at 16.5x emoluments; fully tax-exempt; state rules may vary.
- Renamed header/titles to "India Gratuity (and applicable Taxation) Calculator";
  url-driven nav highlighting (js/active-nav.js).
- Attribution on all pages: Nirav Chhaya + "Developed with the help of opencode
  (model: big-pickle)".
