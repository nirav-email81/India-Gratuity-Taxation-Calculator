These examples assume a flat 30% bracket for simplicity — the calculator above taxes the excess slab-by-slab on (your payout-year income + taxable excess) for the exact amount, reaching the higher brackets automatically.

Important — Before you receive gratuity
  • Prior gratuity declaration: During the gratuity process you must declare whether you have received gratuity before (under this or any other employer). It reduces your lifetime ₹20,00,000 tax-free ceiling.
  • Form I submission: Gratuity claims are submitted via Form I to your employer. The employer must settle the dues within 30 days of them becoming payable.
  • Employer payment: If the employer delays beyond 30 days, they are liable to pay simple interest on the amount.
Who is Eligible for Gratuity in India?
  • Permanent employees: at least 5 years of continuous service (or 4 years + 240 days in the final year under settled precedents), except death or permanent disablement.
  • Fixed-term employees: eligible for pro-rata gratuity after just 1 year of continuous service (new 2025 rule).
  • Third-party/agency contract workers: eligibility follows your actual employer (the staffing agency), not the client.
  • The organization must have 10 or more employees on any day in the preceding 12 months.
  • Gratuity is payable on retirement, resignation, death, disablement, or termination — unless forfeited for misconduct.
